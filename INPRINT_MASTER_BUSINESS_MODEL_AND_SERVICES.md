## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Manifesto

### 1.1 What is INPRINT?
**INPRINT** is a premier **Creative Production Agency and Physical Brand Atelier** based in Lahore, Pakistan. We bridge the critical industry divide between top-tier creative design studios and heavy industrial print factories.

Historically, Pakistani businesses and regional enterprises have faced a persistent dilemma:
1. **Traditional Creative Design Agencies** create brilliant visual identities, brand guidelines, and packaging concepts on screen, but possess zero technical manufacturing knowledge regarding paper calipers, dieline structural engineering, CMYK color gamuts, ink trap values, or substrate-finishing compatibility.
2. **Traditional Commercial Print Shops** operate heavy machinery and offset presses, but lack design sensibility, brand guardianship, typography rigor, and quality assurance, often ruining premium brand identities through crude reproductions and substandard finishing.

**INPRINT unifies both worlds into a single, cohesive hybrid agency.** We take high-growth enterprises, established retail brands, corporate institutions, and hospitality leaders **"From idea to brand launch. Design to delivery."**

* **Average Order Value (AOV):** PKR 150,000 – PKR 1,800,000 per production run.

#### Stream 2: Architectural Signage & Spatial Fabrication (High-Value Project Engine)
* **Description:** 3D fabricated channel letters, backlit halo signage, CNC routed aluminum composite panels, and exhibition spaces.
* **Economic Dynamics:** High transaction values driven by commercial retail expansions, franchise rollouts, and corporate office renovations.
* **AOV:** PKR 250,000 – PKR 3,500,000 per location.

#### Stream 3: Brand Identity & Design Engineering (High-Margin Creative Engine)
* **Description:** Brand design, visual identity systems, bespoke packaging dieline development, and pre-flight color management audits.
* **Economic Dynamics:** Zero material cost; 100% intellectual property and engineering time. Locks clients into INPRINT for subsequent manufacturing runs.
* **AOV:** PKR 120,000 – PKR 650,000 per brand suite.

#### Stream 4: Commercial Collateral & Business Essentials (Predictable Flow)
* **Description:** Premium business cards, presentation folders, corporate stationery suites, annual reports, and brochures.
* **Economic Dynamics:** Short turnaround times (24–72 hours), standard sizes, high order velocity.
* **AOV:** PKR 15,000 – PKR 120,000 per order.

#### Stream 5: Long-Term Enterprise Retainers & Franchise Supply Contracts
* **Description:** Turnkey vendor contracts supplying growing retail and restaurant chains with uniform packaging, staff apparel, and store signage across Pakistan.
* **Economic Dynamics:** Annual contracts with agreed rate cards, guaranteed production windows, and minimum monthly order commitments.

## 5. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP) & Production Pipeline

Every project at INPRINT advances through our **9-Stage Production Quality System**, tracked transparently in real time:

```
[1. Order Placed] ────────▶ [2. Pre-Press Review] ───────▶ [3. Digital Proof]
         │                              │                             │
         ▼                              ▼                             ▼
[4. Client Approval] ─────▶ [5. CTP Plate Setup] ────────▶ [6. Press Printing]
         │                              │                             │
         ▼                              ▼                             ▼
[7. Die-Cut & Assembly] ──▶ [8. Quality Inspection] ─────▶ [9. Nationwide Dispatch]
```

### Stage-by-Stage Breakdown
1. **Order Intake & Job Bag Generation:** The project is entered into the system with unique tracking ID (`INP-XXXX`), paper grammage specifications, finish codes, and delivery deadline.
2. **Pre-Press Digital Forensics:** Automated inspection of artwork:
   - 3mm bleed margin check around all edges.
   - CMYK color space confirmation (FOGRA39 or GRACoL profiles).
   - Minimum 300 DPI image resolution verification at 1:1 reproduction scale.
   - Font outlines converted to vector curves to prevent font substitution.
3. **Soft & Hard Proofing:** Generation of high-resolution calibrated PDF proof for client review. For complex packaging runs, a 1:1 structural unprinted dummy is supplied.
4. **Client Formal Sign-Off:** Digital timestamped sign-off via our portal or signed physical proof.
5. **Computer-to-Plate (CTP) Imaging:** Laser exposure of high-sensitivity aluminum offset plates at 2400 DPI screen frequency.
6. **Press Printing & Color Calibration:** Heidelberg press operators calibrate ink fountain keys using spectrophotometers to guarantee Delta-E values below 2.0.
7. **Post-Press Embellishment & Die-Cutting:** Thermal lamination, foil stamping, high-precision automated die-cutting, folding, and gluing.
8. **Multi-Point Quality Control (QC):** Individual inspection for scuffing, registration alignment, glue integrity, and structural rigidity. Defective units are rejected and reprinted immediately.
9. **Secure Packing & Nationwide Dispatch:** Double-wall corrugated packaging with moisture-barrier film and plastic corner guards. Dispatched via TCS express or specialized transport fleets.
