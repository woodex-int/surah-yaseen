Poster size guide

How big is big?

Bars are the real printed area, drawn to the same scale. A4 is roughly a page from this year's calendar; A0 is nearly a metre by 1.2.

Before you upload

Run the artwork checker
✓What machines are posters printed on? A2 and below run on our HP Indigo 12000 — litho-digital, G7-calibrated, best colour on virtually any paper. A1 and bigger go on the EFI VUTEk h5, a hybrid sheet-fed large-format press reaching 1200 DPI with white ink. Finishing is Kongsberg C24 digital cutting plus a Polar 95i guillotine.
!Where should you put a poster? Indoors, at eye height and in the last five seconds of a walk-past: queue lines, stairwell landings, till counters. Outdoors, put it behind glass or on PVC — an unprotected paper poster in a Lahore June is pulp by Friday.
