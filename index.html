Poster printing, explained properly

From A4 promos to 60×40 that stops traffic

Posters remain the cheapest square foot of attention in Pakistan. A3 costs less than a cup of coffee per week and sits in the window where your customer already stands. Our VUTEk h5 and Indigo 12000 run to 1200 DPI, so you can concentrate on the artwork and let us nail the print.

Not design-ready? Ten free templates sit in the Design Studio →, sized and bleed-set for every stock. Got your own file? Send PDF, PNG or JPG — we will convert to CMYK, add 3mm bleed and tell you in plain words if it will come out soft.

Outdoor in Lahore, Karachi or Punjab's open air means humidity, dust and 44°C glass. That is why every outdoor poster we print leaves the building on 220 or 400-micron PVC with laminate, not paper on a flex job.

iArtwork requirements, short version: 300 DPI at final size, CMYK, 3mm bleed, 5mm safe margin, outlined fonts. PDF/X-1a preferred. We accept up to 200MB and will not judge you for exporting from PowerPoint.
✓No minimum order. One A4 is a real order and gets the same artwork check as 5,000 A0s. Bulk price breaks still start at five.
